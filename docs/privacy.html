Privacy Policy

Deal Scout — Sale Calendar

The short version

Deal Scout is free and does not collect personal data.

What stays on your device

The brands you follow, the sales you save, your reminder settings and your reminders inbox are stored only on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive them and no account system. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location

Deal Scout does not access your location.

Notifications

Sale reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. No push server is involved.

To show newly announced sales, the app downloads a public list of sales from this website (stardust444-eng.github.io) about every 6 hours. The request carries no account, identifier or information about what you follow. Like any website, the host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; we don't receive or use them.

Links to retailers and sources

When you open a retailer’s website or a source article, you leave Deal Scout and that site’s own privacy policy applies. Deal Scout’s links are not affiliate links. If that changes, this policy and the app will say so before it happens.

No tracking

Deal Scout contains no analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking pixels, and we don’t sell or share data, because we don’t collect any.

Children

Deal Scout is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email appsupport47@gmail.com. If you write to us, we use your address only to reply.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. Last updated October 31, 2026.
