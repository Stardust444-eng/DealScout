Reminders aren’t showing up

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, then Deal Scout, and make sure Allow Notifications is on. In Deal Scout, check that notifications are on in Settings and that you follow at least one brand. Reminders never arrive overnight and are capped at two a day.

Where do the sale dates come from?

Every sale shows its source. “Announced” means the retailer published the dates. “Expected” means they’re based on last year’s sale, with the source linked.

Deal Scout is independent and not affiliated with the retailers listed.
